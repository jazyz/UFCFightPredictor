## P9 · #12 Defense features are career-length in disguise: sqrSum applied to an already-summed quantity
*bug · impact high · effort S · CONFIRMED*  
**Where:** process_fights_alpha.py:214-215, 273-274; predict_fights_alpha.py:125-126

Defense accumulates Sum((1-opp_accuracy_i)*i^2), then the feature is computed as sqrSum(defense_sum / totalfights) — a cubic polynomial applied to a quantity that already grows ~N^2 with career length. Measured on data/detailed_fighter_stats.csv: median deployed 'Sig. str.% defense' is 5.2 for fighters with <=4 fights vs 106,666 for >=15 fights — a ~20,000x scale gap driven by fight count, not skill. Six defense pairs (Sig. str.%, Td%, Leg%, Clinch%, Ground%) sit in the deployed 121-column set. Correct form, matching every other feature: defense_sum / sqrSum(totalfights) — a weighted mean of opponent accuracy against the fighter. Both files share the wrong formula, so it is not train/serve skew — the feature itself is broken.

**Mechanism to ROI:** Twelve deployed columns currently re-encode experience (already covered by totalfights/wins) while burying takedown-defense and striking-defense signal the market prices heavily (chin, TDD). Recovering true defense sharpens exactly the picks where the model is overconfident on dogs — a dog pick against a high-defense opponent is where stated 64% hits 40%. Gate the retrain on calibration bands per CLAUDE.md, not accuracy.

**Risks:** Requires full recompute + retrain; corr-prune set will shift, so watch the Red/Blue symmetry guard in ml_ensemble.py.

**Verifier corrections:** The deployed selected_columns.json contains five defense pairs (10 columns: Sig. str.%, Td%, Leg%, Clinch%, Ground%), not "six pairs"/"twelve deployed columns" — the five feature names listed are correct, the counts overstate by one pair. Also, the 5.2 median corresponds specifically to the model-eligible 2-4 fight range; across all fighters with <=4 fights (including 1-fight) it is 1.8, i.e., the scale gap is even larger (~58,000x) than stated.

**Verifier reasoning:** Every checkable claim verified against the repo. (1) Code: process_fights_alpha.py:273-274 accumulates defense as (1-opp_value)*sqr(fights); lines 214-215 emit sqrSum(defense_sum/totalfights); predict_fights_alpha.py:125-126 has the identical form, so the feature is broken identically in train and serve, as claimed. Every sibling feature uses sum/sqrSum(totalfights) (process:210-213, predict:120-123), confirming the proposed fix matches the house pattern. (2) Data: reproduced on data/detailed_fighter_stats.csv — median deployed Sig. str.% defense is 5.2 for fighters with 2-4 fights vs 106,666.2 for >=15 fights, matching the finding to the decimal; Spearman of the deployed feature vs totalfights is 0.986 (it is a career-length counter), vs 0.157 for the corrected form, which sits bounded near 0.5 (true weighted-mean opponent accuracy).
